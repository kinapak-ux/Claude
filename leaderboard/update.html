Update the score sheet

About 2 minutes, on a computer that can open Google Drive. You only need to do this once.

Checking your sheet…
  1. Tap Copy the new script above.
  2. Open your Family leaderboard Google Sheet, the one with the Scores tab.
  3. In the menu, click Extensions then Apps Script. The code opens in a new tab.
  4. Click anywhere in the code, press Ctrl+A (⌘+A on a Mac) to select it all, then Ctrl+V (⌘+V) to paste over it.
  5. Click the 💾 Save icon.
  6. Click the blue Deploy button (top right), then Manage deployments.
  7. Click the ✏️ pencil (Edit) icon.
  8. Under Version, pick New version. Leave everything else as it is.
  9. Click Deploy, then Done. If Google asks for permission, click through and allow it as before.
  10. Come back here and tap Check again.

Use Manage deployments, not New deployment. That way the web address stays the same and the games keep working.
