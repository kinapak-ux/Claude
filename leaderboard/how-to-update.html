Score sheet:
picture guide

This lets the newest games (Family Quiz, Battleships, Snap Race and Times Table Blaster) save scores to the family leaderboard. It takes about 3 minutes, and you only do it once.

Before you start

💻 Use a home computer, not your phone and not a work laptop. Work networks often block Google's script pages.
👤 Sign in to the Google account that made the score sheet.
📱 Tip: open this page on your phone too, so you can follow the pictures while you click on the computer.
🔗 Address for the computer: kinapak-ux.github.io/Claude/leaderboard/how-to-update.html

Checking your sheet…

1Copy the new script

On the computer, click this button. It copies the new script, ready to paste in step 4.

2Open your score sheet

Go to drive.google.com or sheets.google.com and open the spreadsheet you made for the leaderboard. You'll know it's the right one because it has a Scores tab at the bottom, full of game results.

4Paste over the old script

Click anywhere in the code. Press Ctrl+A to select all of it, so it all turns blue. Then press Ctrl+V to paste the new script over it.

10Check it worked

🤔 If something looks different

Google says "Authorisation required" or "Google hasn't verified this app"

That's normal for a script you made yourself. Click Review permissions and choose your account. Then click Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe), then Allow. It only gives your own script access to your own sheet.

There's no pencil, or the list is empty

Make sure you clicked Manage deployments, not Test deployments. If there's more than one item under Active, click the one marked Web app with the oldest date, then the pencil.

I clicked "New deployment" and finished it

No harm done. The games still use the old address. Go to Deploy → Manage deployments, pick the older Web app (the original), and do steps 7 to 9 on that one. You can archive the new one with the 🗄️ icon if you like.

The Check still says "Needs updating"

Wait a minute and check again. If it still says that, make sure you picked New version in step 8. Leaving it on "Version 1" keeps the old script running. Also check the code says const VERSION = 2; after you pasted it.

The Check says it "couldn't reach the sheet"

The network you're on is probably blocking Google's script pages, as many work networks do. Try the check on your phone with Wi-Fi turned off, or on home Wi-Fi.
