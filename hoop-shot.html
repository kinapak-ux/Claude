Power-ups & rules

Scoring

  • +3Swish: the ball drops in without touching the rim or board.
  • +2Bucket: it goes in after a bounce.
  • +1Hit a bird. It also drops the power-up written above it.
  • FireMake 3 in a row and you're on fire: points x2 until you miss.

Birds carry these power-ups

  • WIDEWide Rim: the basket is twice as wide. 3 shots
  • x2Double Points on every basket. 3 shots
  • AIMLaser Sight: the guide shows the whole flight. 3 shots
  • MAGMagnet Rim: the hoop pulls in near misses. 2 shots
  • SLOWSlow Motion: the ball flies at half speed. 3 shots
  • x3Triple Shot: your next throw fires three balls. 1 shot

It gets harder

  • 8After 8 baskets the hoop starts sliding side to side.
  • 15After 15 baskets wind pushes the ball. The aim guide includes it.
