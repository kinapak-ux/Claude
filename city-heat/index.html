CITY HEAT '99
Crime pays. Until it doesn't. · PRESS START
18+ Strong violence, blood, crime and coarse language. Adults only.
Rampage: loaded with every gun, you've got 2 minutes to cause as much damage as you can. Wrecks, kills and smashed cars score points, chain them for up to x5, and every wreck adds 3 seconds.
Goal: make $25,000 any way you can. Ringing payphones (green arrow) hand out work: boosts, hits, getaways, kill frenzies.
Cars: walk up to any car and press CAR (E or Enter). If someone's driving, they're getting dragged out.
Guns: grab weapon crates (Pistol, Uzi, Shotgun, Rockets), plus health and armour. X or Ctrl fires, Q switches. You can shoot out of a car window too.
Heat: every crime earns ★ stars. From two stars the cops shoot to kill. Lose them, or drive into the paint shop ($300). Get stopped next to a cop car and you're busted; run out of health and you're wasted.
Phone: push the joypad the way you want to go and you run that way. In a car, push it towards where you want to drive and the car turns and goes; ◼ brakes and reverses.
Graphics: Tiny City and Mini Characters by Kenney (kenney.nl).
Keys: on foot the arrows / WASD run in that direction. In a car: ↑ gas, ↓ brake / reverse, ← → steer. Space slide, E car, X fire, Q weapon, H horn, P pause, M sound.
