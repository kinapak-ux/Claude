Board School

Learn two of the oldest games in the world, one step at a time.

Backgammon

A race with dice. Move all 15 checkers home and off the board before the computer does, and knock its lonely checkers back to the start.

Go

Take turns placing stones to surround empty space. Capture stones by taking away all their breathing room. Simple rules, endless depth.
