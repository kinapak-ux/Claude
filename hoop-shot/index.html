Power-ups & rules

Scoring

  • +3Swish: the ball drops in without touching the rim or board.
  • +2Bucket: it goes in after a bounce.
  • TapMissed? Once the ball hits the ground, tap anywhere to get it back straight away.
  • +1Hit a bird. It also drops the power-up written above it.
  • FireMake 3 in a row and you're on fire: points x2 until you miss.
  • RecordYour longest streak ever, in any mode.
  • 60s60-second challenge: score as many points as you can before the clock runs out. It starts on your first shot.

Birds carry these power-ups

  • WIDEWide Rim: the basket is twice as wide. 3 shots
  • x2Double Points on every basket. 3 shots
  • AIMLaser Sight: the guide shows the whole flight. 3 shots
  • MAGMagnet Rim: the hoop pulls in near misses. 2 shots
  • SLOWSlow Motion: the ball flies at half speed. 3 shots
  • x3Triple Shot: your next throw fires three balls. 1 shot

Other modes

  • FuseHot potato: every ball has a lit fuse. Score before it burns down or it blows up. The fuse gets shorter as your streak grows.
  • SmashDemolition: smash crates and pumpkins for +3 each and clear the set for +5. Explosions take out anything nearby, and a shot that smashes something never counts as a miss.

It gets harder

  • 20Reach a streak of 20 and the hoop starts sliding slowly side to side. It speeds up the longer your streak runs and stops when you miss.
  • 15After 15 baskets wind pushes the ball. The aim guide includes it.
