This uses your camera and compass to draw the satellites where they really are, even the ones you can't see. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

The Sun is shown too, so you can check the compass lines up. On a computer, drag to look around.

      Loading orbit data…

      Positions are worked out live on your phone from the latest published orbit data (CelesTrak) using the SGP4 model, accurate to a few kilometres. "Might be visible" means the satellite is lit by the Sun while your sky is dark, so it could show up as a moving star. Bigger ones like the ISS are easy; small ones need dark skies.
