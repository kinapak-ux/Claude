Turn a photo into a 3D model

Pick a photo and an AI works out how far away each part is, then builds a 3D model you can spin, save, or 3D print.

  • Or drag a photo onto this box, or paste one.
  • Best results: one clear subject (a face, a pet, a statue, a building), well lit.
  • Your photo never leaves your device. The first AI run downloads the model once (about 25 MB).
  • A single photo only shows one side, so you get a raised “relief” of what the camera saw, not the back.
